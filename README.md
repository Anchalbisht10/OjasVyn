OjasVyn — Rural Healthcare Access

"Healthcare is not a privilege. It is a right that distance should never deny."

Built by: Anchal Bisht · anchal001bisht@gmail.com · LinkedIn

The Problem
70% of India's population lives in rural areas served by only 20% of the nation's doctors. People travel over 100 kilometres just to reach a clinic — spending 70–80% of their household budget on the journey alone. Children under 5 die from treatable diseases because a hospital is too far. Women need financial permission just for a basic medical visit.
Healthcare access in rural India is not a knowledge problem. It is a distance, cost, and awareness problem.

What OjasVyn Is
OjasVyn is a bilingual Hindi/English health guidance platform built for rural India. The name combines two Sanskrit words — Ojas (ओजस्), meaning vital energy and physical health, and Vyn, meaning network and connection.
It is a zero-cost, zero-doctor-required first-level guidance tool accessible on any phone.
Users select their symptoms and receive:

Ayurvedic remedies for common conditions
Information about nearby government hospitals and health centres
Guidance in their own language — Hindi or English
AI-powered health companion for follow-up questions

No app download required. No registration. No cost. Ever.

Who It Is Built For

Rural communities across India, especially Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh
Women who cannot easily access or afford medical visits
Low-income families who avoid hospitals because one visit consumes most of their monthly income
Anyone who needs first-level health guidance without travelling hours to find it
